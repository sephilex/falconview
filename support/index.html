Contact Support

Questions about the app, a notification, or your privacy? Contact the developer, Sephilex.

sephilex@gmail.com

For app issues

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and your app and iOS versions. A screenshot with private details removed can help. Please do not send passwords, login tokens, or private camera footage.

For privacy requests

You can ask about, access, correct or request deletion of information associated with your notification installation. Tell us which action you need; we will help verify the request and identify the relevant records without asking you to email a password or token.

You can also turn off receiving in the app, remove a saved connection, or change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Historical relay records are not all automatically removed by those actions. Your own Frigate’s recordings and registrations are managed by its administrator. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FalconView is an independent client for Frigate.
