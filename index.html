FalconView Pro

All features are free on the demo server, and you can add one own server for free. On your own servers, camera, event and search detail pages, exports, notification settings, Live Activities, Home Screen widgets and additional servers require FalconView Pro.

Choose a lifetime unlock (one-time purchase), or a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription. Free trials are available to eligible subscribers; current prices and trial terms are shown in the App Store before purchase. Family Sharing is supported, and Restore Purchases is built into the app.

Live video, recordings, widget snapshots, search and exports connect directly to the Frigate server you choose. Optional push notifications and Live Activities use a notification relay; details are available in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and access to a Frigate 0.17 or later server, or the public demo. Remote access requires a reachable server address. Available features depend on your Frigate version, server configuration, camera capabilities and permissions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Home Assistant?

No. FalconView connects directly to your Frigate server without requiring Home Assistant or a FalconView account.

How do push notifications work?

Live video, recordings, search and exports connect directly to your Frigate server, and your credentials stay in the iOS Keychain. Optional push notifications are delivered through a notification relay. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How does remote access work?

Remote access requires a reachable server address from outside your local network. You can configure separate local and remote addresses for each Frigate server you add.

Is FalconView affiliated with Frigate?

No. FalconView is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Frigate project.
